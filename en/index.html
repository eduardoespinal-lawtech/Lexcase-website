FAQ

Clear answers about Lexbell.

Everything about our local privacy model, licensing, performance, and cross-platform compatibility.

Lexbell is a single-user desktop software for independent lawyers, immigration case workers, and rental managers. Manage cases, court deadlines, documents, and clients 100% locally and encrypted on your Mac or Windows computer without cloud reliance or per-user seat fees.

You can start completely free with up to 50 active cases, with no time limits and no credit card required. For unlimited case files and advanced features, affordable monthly or annual plans are available without per-seat price gouging.

Unlike Clio or web-based practice management platforms that charge expensive per-user monthly subscriptions and store confidential client files on remote servers, Lexbell runs locally on your machine, guarantees attorney-client privilege by design, works 100% offline, and costs a fraction of the price.

Lexbell runs on a Local-First architecture: all your information lives exclusively on your computer and never leaves it or gets sent to external servers. All matter management, deadline calculation, and document tracking features work 100% offline without an internet connection.
